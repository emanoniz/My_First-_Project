ABOUT CORTIS

CORTIS is a five-member boy group under BIGHIT MUSIC. The group consists of MARTIN, JAMES, JUHOON, SEONGHYEON, and KEONHO. Their name comes from six letters of the phrase “COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES.” It represents the idea of thinking freely and creating beyond the limits set by the world. CORTIS is described as a young creator crew. The members work together on music, choreography, performances, and video content.
