01 / Introduction

WHO ARE
CORTIS?

CORTIS is a five-member South Korean boy group under BIGHIT MUSIC, a label within HYBE.


The group consists of MARTIN, JAMES, JUHOON, SEONGHYEON and KEONHO.


Their name is created from six letters taken from the phrase "COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES." It represents the idea of thinking freely and not being limited by the rules and expectations of the world.


CORTIS is known as a creative crew. The members participate in different parts of their artistic work, including songwriting, composition, choreography and video production.

02 / Group history

THE BEGINNING
OF CORTIS.

CORTIS began its journey in 2025 under BIGHIT MUSIC. Their debut introduced a new creative direction built around music, performance and visual storytelling.

AUGUST 18, 2025

Official Debut

CORTIS officially debuted with the lead single "What You Want".

SEPTEMBER 8, 2025

First EP — COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES

Their first EP was released with five tracks: GO!, What You Want, FaSHioN, JoyRide and Lullaby.

THEIR CREATIVE APPROACH

More Than Performers

The members take part in creative processes such as songwriting, composition, choreography and video work.
